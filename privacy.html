2. Information we collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:

  • valid identification details and, where required for accommodation or legal compliance, a copy of an identification document;

Please provide accurate information and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in general enquiry messages.

3. Why and how we use personal information

We process personal information only for specified purposes, including to:

  • answer enquiries and prepare, confirm, administer, modify, or cancel bookings;
  • provide accommodation, restaurant, diving, training, excursion, and related guest services;
  • verify identity, diving certification, age, medical fitness, and participation requirements;
  • protect the health and safety of guests, staff, and other divers and respond to emergencies;
  • process payments, issue invoices, keep accounting records, and comply with legal obligations;
  • handle feedback, complaints, disputes, and legal claims;
  • operate, secure, measure, and improve the website and our services; and
  • publish guest photos or videos for social media or promotional purposes when permission has been requested.

Depending on the situation, processing is based on the performance of a booking or service contract, steps taken at your request before a booking, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of life and health, our legitimate business interests, or your consent where Philippine law requires it. Medical information is limited to assessing diving fitness and supporting safe participation.

We do not currently use guest information for promotional email or message campaigns, and we do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Who may receive personal information

The Resort and Dive Shop share relevant information with each other when needed to coordinate your stay, booking, payment, diving activity, or safety. Access is limited to staff who need the information for their work, including authorised reception and diving staff.

We may also disclose limited information to:

  • Booking.com, Airbnb, or another booking platform you use;
  • SSI Philippines and the MySSI service for diver profiles, training records, and certification;
  • Google Drive for booking records and Meta Messenger for reception communications;
  • banks and payment services used for your transaction, including GCash, Wise, Remitly, and PayPal;
  • Netlify for website hosting and form processing, and Google Tag Manager or connected analytics services when analytics consent is enabled;
  • local transport or tour providers, normally limited to your name and relevant arrival or departure information;
  • accountants, professional advisers, insurers, emergency or medical responders, and public authorities when reasonably necessary or legally required.

Some providers may process information outside the Philippines. Their handling of information is also governed by their own privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Records may be held in an Excel booking file on Google Drive, reception Messenger conversations, the MySSI service, email accounts, and paper files stored in our office. We use reasonable organisational, physical, and technical measures to limit unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Our standard retention periods are:

  • Diving records and forms: up to five years, after which local records are securely disposed of. Records held in MySSI may remain subject to SSI's own retention requirements.
  • Other booking, enquiry, identification, and operational records: generally up to one year after the stay, activity, or closure of the enquiry, unless a longer period is required for a dispute or by law.
  • Invoices and accounting records: five years, calculated in accordance with applicable Philippine tax-record requirements.

When a retention period ends, paper records are securely destroyed and electronic records are deleted or anonymised where reasonably possible. Backup copies and records controlled by third-party platforms may remain for their normal deletion cycle or legal retention period.

7. Your data privacy rights

Subject to the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its implementing rules, you may have the right to:

  • be informed about the processing of your personal information;
  • request access to and a copy of information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • object to certain processing or withdraw consent where consent is the basis;
  • request erasure or blocking where the legal conditions are met;
  • request data portability where applicable;
  • claim damages and lodge a complaint with the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Some requests may be limited by safety, contractual, accounting, certification, legal-claim, or other lawful retention requirements. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity before acting on a request.
